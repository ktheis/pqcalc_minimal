PQcalc Tutorials & Examples

                
            

            

                
Click "Try This Example" to insert any scenario directly into your calculation workspace.


                

                    

                        
1. Quantities & Basic Math

                        
Assign quantities with names, numbers, and units. Do arithmetic naturally.

                        
length = 5.0 m
width = 3.2 m
area = length * width

                        
                    


                    

                        
2. Unit Conversions ('in')

                        
Convert calculated results into any compatible units.

                        
d = 120 km
t = 1.5 h
speed = d / t
speed in m/s

                        
                    


                    

                        
3. Uncertainty Propagation

                        
Enter values with uncertainties in least significant digits, e.g. 10.5(2). PQcalc tracks error propagation and significant figures.

                        
x = 10.5(2) m
t = 2.0(1) s
v = x / t

                        
                    


                    

                        
4. Chemistry & Molar Masses

                        
Look up molar masses with M[Formula] and label samples with bracketed formulas like m[CaCl2] and n[CaCl2].

                        
m[CaCl2] = 50.0 g
n[CaCl2] = m[CaCl2] / M[CaCl2]

                        
                    


                    

                        
5. Chemical Reaction Equations

                        
Prefix reaction equations with ! to check balancing and stoichiometry.

                        
! 2 H2 + O2 -> 2 H2O
! 4Fe + 3O2 -> 2Fe2O3

                        
                    


                    

                        
6. Converting 'using' Relations

                        
Use known relations to convert between chemical amount and mass or volume.

                        
c_sol = 0.50 mol/L
V_sol = 25.0 mL
n_sol = c_sol * V_sol
c_sol using M
